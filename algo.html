How the topics connect

Analysis, asymptotics, complexity and case-analysis are one toolkit for reasoning about cost. Divide & conquer, greedy and dynamic programming are the three core design paradigms. Traversals, graph search, MST and shortest paths apply those paradigms to graphs — Kruskal is greedy, Bellman-Ford and Floyd-Warshall are DP, and merge sort is divide & conquer.
