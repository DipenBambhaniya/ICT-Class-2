How the topics connect

SDLC models and design principles frame how software gets built at all. Agile methods offer an alternative, iterative way to run that process. Testing and project management (including SLAs) verify the result and keep delivery on track. And software quality models — together with a domain example like SCADA, where failures have real physical consequences — tie the whole discipline back to why rigor matters.
