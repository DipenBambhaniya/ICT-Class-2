How the topics connect

Mission Mode Projects and national digital platforms are WHAT gets built — the concrete e-governance systems and shared infrastructure citizens and departments actually use. Policy (NeGP, Digital India, and at the state level Gujarat's IT/ITeS Policy 2022-27) decides WHY and WHERE investment flows, setting objectives and incentives. Organizations like MeitY, NIC, C-DAC, STQC, UIDAI and their Gujarat counterparts GIL and GESIA are WHO actually plans, builds, certifies and runs that infrastructure. And the legislative framework — the IT Act, DPDP Act, Aadhaar Act, Copyright Act and RTI Act — is what gives all of it legal force, from recognizing an electronic record as valid evidence to protecting the personal data every one of these platforms collects.
