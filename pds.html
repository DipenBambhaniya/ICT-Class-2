How the topics connect

Arrays and linked lists are the two primitive containers; stacks and queues are restricted views built on them. Trees generalize linked lists to branching structure; BSTs add ordering and heaps add the heap-order property. Graphs are the most general — trees are just connected acyclic graphs — and BFS/DFS reuse the queue and stack.
