How the topics connect

Threats and attacks describe what can go wrong; data security/privacy/protection describes what's at stake and how to defend it. Risk management turns that understanding into a structured, ongoing process — supported nationally in India by CERT-In. Cyber law and governance (IT Act 2000, national policy, and the CISO role) supply the legal accountability and organizational leadership that make all of the above effective and sustained.
