How the topics connect

File organization and indexes decide where data sits on disk; B+ trees are the structure that makes indexes scale. Transactions, schedules and concurrency control are one chain: a transaction is the unit of work, a schedule is how several are interleaved, and concurrency control is the mechanism that keeps every schedule serializable and recoverable.
