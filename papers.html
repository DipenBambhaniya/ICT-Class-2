About these papers

Each page is a full transcription of GPSC's official Provisional Answer Key PDF — the exact question paper GPSC publishes after a Preliminary Test, together with the answer key it proposes before the objection window closes (answers can be revised after candidate objections, so treat them as GPSC's provisional position, not a final verified key). These are reproduced for personal study/reference; they are GPSC's own exam content, not questions authored for this site.
