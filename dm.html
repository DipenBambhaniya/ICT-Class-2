How the topics connect

Logic underpins proof and specification. Sets, relations and functions are the vocabulary for everything else — an equivalence relation partitions, a partial order structures. Lattices are special posets used in program analysis and Boolean algebra. Groups formalise symmetry and modular arithmetic (crypto). Graph theory and combinatorics supply the counting and structural results behind algorithm analysis.
