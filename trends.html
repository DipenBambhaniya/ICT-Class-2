How the topics connect

AI/ML and Generative AI are increasingly the "brains" applied on top of the data and infrastructure the other topics generate — including the massive, continuous data streams produced by IoT devices and processed on cloud-native infrastructure. Blockchain offers a complementary, decentralised way to establish trust and tamper-evidence for records and transactions in that same digital ecosystem. And 5G/6G, quantum computing, and AR/VR/Metaverse represent the next layer of connectivity, computation, and interface technologies that will carry and shape how all of these capabilities reach citizens, businesses and government services in the years ahead.
