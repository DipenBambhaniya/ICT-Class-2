How to use this site

  1. Pick a unit from the sidebar and read each topic page — plain-language notes, a diagram, a worked example and exam traps.
  2. Do the inline MCQs on each page, then hit "Practice all 50" to drill that topic in the quiz.
  3. Review explanations — every question shows why the correct answer is right, immediately and again in the final review list.
  4. Repeat — questions shuffle every attempt, so retaking keeps you on your toes.
