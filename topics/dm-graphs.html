Graph Theory

A graph is vertices joined by edges. The classic results: the handshaking lemma, trees, bipartiteness and colouring, Euler vs Hamilton, matchings (Hall, König), planarity (Euler's formula, Kuratowski) and connectivity.

Key points

Handshaking lemma
Σ deg(v) = 2|E|. So the number of odd-degree vertices is even. K_n has C(n,2) edges.
Trees
n vertices, n−1 edges, connected and acyclic (any 2 imply the 3rd). Unique path between any two vertices. Labeled spanning trees of K_n: n^(n−2) (Cayley).
Colouring
χ(G) = min colours for a proper vertex colouring. χ = 2 ⇔ bipartite ⇔ no odd cycle. Greedy uses ≤ Δ+1; planar graphs ≤ 4 (Four Colour Theorem).
Euler vs Hamilton
Euler circuit (every edge once): all degrees even — polynomial test. Hamiltonian cycle (every vertex once): NP-complete.
Matchings & planarity
Hall's theorem: matching saturating X ⇔ |N(S)| ≥ |S| ∀ S ⊆ X. König: max matching = min vertex cover (bipartite). Euler: V − E + F = 2; simple planar ⇒ E ≤ 3V − 6.

Example

Handshaking: a graph with degree sequence (3, 3, 3, 3, 2) — is it possible?
  Σ deg = 14 = 2|E| ⇒ |E| = 7.  OK (sum is even).
  Degree sequence (3, 3, 3, 1): Σ = 10 ⇒ |E| = 5, and 3 odd-degree vertices —
  IMPOSSIBLE, the count of odd-degree vertices must be even.

Euler's formula: K_4 drawn planar has V=4, E=6, F=4 → 4 − 6 + 4 = 2. ✓
K_5: E = 10 > 3·5 − 6 = 9 ⇒ NOT planar.
Common exam traps

Euler circuit (edges) needs all-even degrees — a polynomial test; Hamiltonian cycle (vertices) is NP-complete. χ(G) = 2 requires the graph to have an edge AND be bipartite. The number of odd-degree vertices is always even. E ≤ 3V − 6 only for SIMPLE planar graphs with V ≥ 3 (bipartite planar: E ≤ 2V − 4).

Practice MCQs

Q1. The sum of all vertex degrees in an undirected graph equals:

  1. |V|
  2. |E|
  3. 2|E| (handshaking lemma)
  4. |V| + |E|
Show answer

Answer: C. The number of odd-degree vertices is therefore even.

Q2. A tree on n vertices has exactly:

  1. n edges
  2. n − 1 edges, connected and acyclic
  3. 2n edges
  4. n/2 edges
Show answer

Answer: B. Any two of {connected, acyclic, n−1 edges} imply the third.

Q3. A graph is BIPARTITE iff:

  1. it has an even number of vertices
  2. it contains no odd-length cycle (2-colourable)
  3. it is connected
  4. it is a tree
Show answer

Answer: B. Trees and even cycles are bipartite.

Q4. An Euler circuit exists in a connected graph iff:

  1. every vertex has even degree
  2. it is bipartite
  3. it has n−1 edges
  4. every vertex has odd degree
Show answer

Answer: A. An Euler PATH exists iff 0 or 2 vertices have odd degree.

Q5. Euler's formula for a connected planar graph: V − E + F equals:

  1. 0
  2. 1
  3. 2
  4. V
Show answer

Answer: C. F counts faces including the outer face; hence E ≤ 3V − 6.
