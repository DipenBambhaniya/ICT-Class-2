How the topics connect

A data centre must first keep data safe and reachable (storage & availability), then run workloads efficiently on shared hardware (virtualization & pipelines), decide where those workloads should physically live (public/private/hybrid cloud), and finally keep the resulting service both fast to grow and dependable under failure (scalability & reliability).
